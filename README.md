BOB — Bismillah Online Business

A TCprojects Digital Commerce Project

BOB (Bismillah Online Business) is an online commerce platform developed by TCprojects, with an initial focus on making everyday shopping more accessible to the local community in Cape Town, South Africa.

The platform brings together digital product browsing, online ordering, inventory administration, order management and customer communication in one commerce experience.

Project Overview

BOB was developed to address the growing need for convenient, accessible online shopping for local communities.

Initially focused on Roosendal and Delft in Cape Town, the project provides a digital retail experience for everyday products, including groceries, food, beverages, snacks and household essentials.

The platform combines a customer-facing storefront with administrative tools for managing products, stock, orders, promotions and day-to-day commerce operations.

BOB's longer-term direction is to evolve into a broader multi-category online commerce platform supported by supplier partnerships and expanded fulfilment capabilities.

BOB is part of the TCprojects portfolio of digital platforms and technology-driven business ventures.

Product Verification and Checkout Protection

A publication and checkout safety milestone was implemented in November 2026.

The documented safeguards include:

- Products require verified pricing, stock and images before publication.
- Products priced at zero or less cannot be published.
- Changes to product pricing, stock or media reset the relevant approval.
- Product verification is restricted to the owner account.
- Zero-priced products are excluded from cart preview calculations.
- Order creation rejects zero-priced products using database-backed pricing.

The implementation report recorded 12 successful tests. Final browser verification of the owner-facing approval controls was still outstanding at the time of documentation.

Supplier Catalogue Import Approach

BOB's catalogue preparation work has established a controlled approach to adding supplier products.

The intended workflow is designed around:

1. Extracting product information from supplier catalogues.
2. Staging records for administrative review.
3. Representing individual flavours as separate products where applicable.
4. Reviewing and approving product images manually.
5. Entering retail selling prices separately.
6. Confirming available stock.
7. Publishing products only after the required approvals.

Supplier catalogue prices are not intended to become BOB retail selling prices or product cost records through this import process.

The catalogue import interface and full import workflow remain planned development work.
